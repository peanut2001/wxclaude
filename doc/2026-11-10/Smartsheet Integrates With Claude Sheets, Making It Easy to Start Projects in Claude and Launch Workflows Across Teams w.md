It becomes part of the [Execution Graph](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.smartsheet.com%2Fcontent-center%2Fproduct-insights%2Fproduct-updates%2Fengage-product-innovation%3Futm_source%3Dbusinesswire%26utm_medium%3Dreferral%26utm_campaign%3Dpub-glob-press-release-claude-sheets&esheet=54639020&newsitemid=20261110763156&lan=en-US&anchor=Execution+Graph&index=6&md5=77a26d7d4134d73b73cbf775d750ff79), which maps how work connects across the business, from tasks to projects to programs to strategic goals. Built on 20 years of workflow intelligence from millions of projects, the Execution Graph knows an organization's project history, the way its work gets done, how its teams work together, and the systems and data they rely on every day. And that context compounds: each discussion, dashboard, automation and agent added around the sheet gives the next one more to work from, so the work becomes more valuable the longer it runs in Smartsheet. For customers, that means:

*   **Work that has an owner and a place in the plan.** A tracker that began in a conversation is tied to the project, program and goal it serves, so people can see who is responsible and how it affects other work.

*   **AI agents that work with real context.**Smartsheet's Creation Agent turns a plain-language goal into sheets, dashboards, reports and automations. The Data Quality Agent flags issues by severity, and Agentic Workflows turn a plain-language request into a recurring task, so a sheet originating from Claude can be checked for data issues or summarized each week on a schedule the team sets.

*   **Earlier warning on problems.** With work connected, leaders can spot an overloaded team or a weak link between projects before it becomes a problem, and [vsActual](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.smartsheet.com%2Fvsactual%3Futm_source%3Dbusinesswire%26utm_medium%3Dreferral%26utm_campaign%3Dpub-glob-press-release-claude-sheets&esheet=54639020&newsitemid=20261110763156&lan=en-US&anchor=vsActual&index=7&md5=772d9a5053fe9b7590a362871005fb19), unveiled at [ENGAGE 2026](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.smartsheet.com%2Fengage%3Futm_source%3Dbusinesswire%26utm_medium%3Dreferral%26utm_campaign%3Dpub-glob-press-release-claude-sheets&esheet=54639020&newsitemid=20261110763156&lan=en-US&anchor=ENGAGE+2026&index=8&md5=2ad78e5d4575955154b53c30351f3c25), shows whether work is on track against priorities.
