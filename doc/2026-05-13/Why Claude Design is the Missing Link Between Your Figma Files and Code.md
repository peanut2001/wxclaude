## Limitations to Be Aware Of

While Claude Design offers a powerful suite of tools, there are some limitations to consider:

*   It is a web-only platform, with no desktop application currently available.
*   The credit-based usage model may restrict access to advanced features for users on lower-tier plans.
*   Credit consumption varies depending on task complexity, requiring careful planning to optimize usage.

These constraints may pose challenges for users with limited budgets or those seeking offline functionality, making strategic planning essential for maximizing the platform’s potential.
